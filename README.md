# SHS Atlas (beta)

A student-made 3D map of Adlai E. Stevenson High School in Lincolnshire, IL. It shows every building and level, every numbered room, hallways and walls, and gives walking directions from any room to any other room.

**Try it:** https://shsatlas.pages.dev (beta, password protected)

> SHS Atlas is not an official Stevenson High School or District 125 website.

## What it does

- Search any room by number or name (7026, nurse, Field House…)
- Walking directions between rooms, with an elevators-only option
- Plan my day: route all your classes at once
- Three ways into the pool: through either locker room, or the deck entrance after 3:30
- Levels 1–4, including the rooftop greenhouse
- Works on phones and iPads: one finger moves, two fingers zoom and turn, three fingers tilt
- Light and dark mode

## Where's the code?

The source code lives in a separate **private** repository, `banxxrr/SHS-Atlas`. This public repo is only here so people can see that the project exists, read about it, and report problems.

The code is private because **a login is coming.** If SHS Atlas goes public, it will get a sign-in so only Stevenson students and staff can use it. That only works if the map can't just be downloaded from GitHub and opened without signing in.

## Found a mistake?

Tap the room on the map, choose **Edit label**, then **Copy my corrections**, and paste them into a new issue here. Wrong room numbers, missing rooms and bad directions are all helpful.

## Version history

- **v0.8** (Oct 28, 2026, current beta): Much faster and stops crashing on phones. New touch controls like a maps app. Directions now start from straight above you, facing where you're going. Fixed the flickering ground around the stadium.
- **v0.7**: School colors and the Patriots logo. Red route line. Darker walls so you can tell where you can't walk.
- **v0.6**: New look: search bar on top, floor buttons on the side, swipe-up panel, dark mode.
- **v0.5**: Walking directions, an elevators-only option, Plan my day, and pool routes.
- **v0.4**: Level 3 added from the district campus map and checked against door photos. Level 4 (rooftop greenhouse) added.
- **v0.3**: West Building levels 1 and 2 redone from the campus map. Hallways and walls marked.
- **v0.2**: Rooms traced from the official floor plans for East levels 1–2 and the Link.
- **v0.1**: First version: the outside of the campus only (buildings, parking lots, fields).

## Beta notes

It may be slow on older phones, and some rooms may be in the wrong spot. Room info comes from the district's 2023–24 campus map and floor plans.
